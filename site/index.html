The argument

AGI claims need more than one score

Across the papers, progress is treated as a combination of capability breadth, transfer to unfamiliar tasks, sustained execution, and reliability under constraints. A strong result on one benchmark supports a claim about that setup; it does not establish that AGI has been achieved.

01

Measure profiles

Report capability by task family, including weak areas and run-to-run variation. Aggregate scores can hide important gaps.

02

Test transfer

Vary task composition, tools, and environments. Record the help and adaptation budget needed to succeed.

03

Inspect the process

Keep action traces, evidence links, constraints, recovery, and cost visible alongside final outcomes.

Method and provenance

Read the evidence in context

The review is an independent, focused literature synthesis of the twenty PDFs in the local corpus. It distinguishes peer-reviewed conference papers from preprints, laboratory reports, position papers, and surveys. Figures 1 from ACL Anthology papers [R10]–[R20] are reproduced with their source captions and attribution under the applicable CC BY 4.0 policy; the other source diagrams are redrawn as reviewer schematics. The review summarizes source prose in original wording and links to each official record. See the ACL copyright policy.

“Prepared for Dr. Vidya Mohanty” identifies the intended academic reader. It does not claim co-authorship, supervision, endorsement, or a year of work. The report is a research resource, not a peer-reviewed publication.
